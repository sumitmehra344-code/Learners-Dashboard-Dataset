Key Strategic Recommendations

Actionable initiatives to optimize balance-sheet working capital and stabilize revenue foundations.

1

Rationalize Working Capital & Liquidate Stagnant Inventory

Implement structured promotional bundling and secondary distributor clearances for low-velocity catalog lines (specifically Trains at 34.7% margin and 2.0% sales). Free up portions of the $30.53M held in warehouse stock to align storage capacity with true order velocity.

2

Launch Targeted Reactivation Campaign for Dormant Accounts

Direct regional sales reps toward the 24 registered clients (19.7% of total portfolio) that have never placed an initial order. Deploy introductory volume incentives focused on proven anchor SKUs (Classic Cars) to acquire immediate recurring revenue at low acquisition friction.

3

Diversify Geographic and Key Account Exposure

Mitigate vulnerability from the top two clients (14.7% share) and top 3 regions (56% share) by rolling out tiered volume pricing to mid-tier distributors, simultaneously accelerating outbound expansion across underserved EMEA and APAC territories.
